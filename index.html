StayPut

Theft alarm for MacBooks with Apple silicon

Turn StayPut on before you step away in a library or café. It locks the screen and watches the MacBook's motion sensor, display hinge and lid. A small nudge shows a warning; picking it up sounds a siren.

Getting started

  • Click the shield in the menu bar and choose Protect This MacBook, or press ⌃⌥⌘L.
  • To turn it off, touch Touch ID, or click Use password and enter your Mac password.
  • Adjust sensitivity, siren volume and duration in Settings. Nudge the MacBook while Settings is open to see the live meter.

Questions

The siren stopped after closing the lid. macOS puts a MacBook to sleep about 25 seconds after the lid closes. Opening it again shows the warning.

No motion sensor found. StayPut needs a MacBook with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). Desktop Macs and Intel Macs have no motion sensor.

I can't unlock. Use your Mac login password via “Use password”. As a last resort, hold the power button for 10 seconds.
