Privacy Policy

StayPut · Last updated November 1, 2026

StayPut does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Motion and lid sensor readings are processed only on your Mac, in real time. They are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your Mac password and Touch ID are checked by macOS. StayPut never stores them.
  • StayPut has no accounts, analytics, advertising or network connections.
  • Your settings are stored locally on your Mac.
